- These experiments synthesize faces from StyleGAN3 pretrained on FFHQ; they do not load real FFHQ photographs or invert them. There is no encoder or supervised smile-classifier train/validation split. Factual IDs are generator random draws, not real-person IDs.
- Generator: `/mnt/big_disk/z.cao/StyleGAN_pkl/stylegan3-r-ffhq-1024x1024.pkl`, key `G_ema`, 1024×1024 RGB in [-1,1], constant noise, no truncation. Source is the bundled `models/generative_models/StyleGAN/stylegan3/`.

- Classifier: original OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32 with literal prompts `["smiling face", "face"]`. Softmax index0 is smiling; index1 is generic face, operationally called non-smiling in the original selection. This is a prompt-relative pseudo-label, not a ground-truth smile annotation. No dedicated smile fine-tuned checkpoint was identified.
